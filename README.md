# Ex11 - Add a Paragraph

## Student Assignment

Complete the San Joaquin Valley Town Hall Scott Sampson webpage.

### Task
Add the missing fourth biography paragraph inside the `<article>` element.

The paragraph must begin with:

> In addition to his museum and laboratory-based studies...

It should contain the supplied Scott Sampson information about his paleontological work in Zimbabwe, South Africa, Madagascar, the United States and Canada, his Discovery Channel work, and his book *Dinosaur Odyssey*.
